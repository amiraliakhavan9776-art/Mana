# Mana AI — Strategy, R&D, Branding and Creative Studios
Status: approved product requirements, **not implemented**.

## Scope
Industry-agnostic modules. Construction company is first pilot, not a limitation.

### Mana Strategy Architect
Universal profession discovery interview; market and competitor research; positioning; business, brand, product and project strategy; goals, KPIs, scenarios, resources, budgets, roadmaps, accountable owners, monitoring and evidence-based revisions. Separate facts from assumptions; cite evidence.

### Mana R&D Lab
Problem definition, hypothesis and literature/market research, idea screening, feasibility, experiment design, prototypes, test results, cost-benefit analysis, product roadmap and versioned decisions. Human review for regulated or safety-critical experiments.

### Mana Brand Studio
Brand discovery, positioning, architecture, naming, tagline, visual/verbal identity, brand guidelines, campaign planning, customer journeys, creative briefs, cross-channel consistency and approval workflows.

### Mana Scent Lab
Signature ambient/personal fragrance briefs; olfactory accords; material sourcing by geography; candidate formulas with concentrations, substitutions, cost, safety constraints, stability and panel-test records. Never represent an untested formula as safe; require IFRA/local compliance and professional validation before production.

### Mana Music Studio
Brand sonic identity, melodies, arrangements, lyrics, instrumentation, prompt-to-audio via locally deployable licensed generation engines, editable stems where available, music licensing and provenance tracking, review and versioning.

### Mana Creative Studio
Image, video, motion, storyboards, product visuals, campaign copy and production pipeline via locally deployable licensed models and editing tools. Preserve rights, provenance and human approval for external publication.

## Integrated example
User: “Create a quiet-luxury identity for my architecture company.”
1. Interview and evidence-based positioning.
2. Brand strategy and creative direction.
3. Coordinated logo/visual identity, fragrance brief and test candidates, sonic identity, marketing materials.
4. Present alternatives, cost/risk/licensing/safety notes, editable deliverables and approval gates.
5. Monitor brand performance and revise.

## Architecture
Orchestrated specialist tools and models under Mana Intelligence Engine; data/tenant isolation, permissioned actions, audit, self-hosted Iran-local baseline, optional overseas compute only with explicit authorized data routing. No foreign service required for core runtime.

## Delivery phases
(1) structured workflows and templates; (2) local retrieval and research tools; (3) creative model integrations; (4) experiments, quality evaluation and autonomous draft iteration; (5) production-ready governed outputs.

## Acceptance
End-to-end pilot produces a coherent strategy, R&D plan, brandbook, scent specification and music creative brief, each with source/provenance, editable exports, versioning, approvals, and honest labeling of what was generated versus actually tested.
